The framework

Nothing is funded once. Everything is funded four ways.

Every clean energy project can carry four kinds of funding at once. Knowing which four is what gets it built.

1

Grant

DCEO · IFA federal awards · USDA

Never repaid. Almost always pays for the part nobody else will touch: predevelopment, engineering, community engagement, staff time.

2

Incentive & rebate

IPA · ComEd · Ameren · Nicor

Paid per unit of output or savings. Illinois Shines pays fifteen years of REC value up front. Utility rebates pay on installed measures.

3

Debt

IFA Climate Bank · enrolled lenders · ESPC

Repaid, but on terms nobody gets on the open market. SSBCI buys the rate to 2%. C-PACE moves repayment onto the tax bill.

4

Tax

IRS · client's CPA

30% of eligible cost under Section 48E, plus depreciation. Tax-exempt owners take it as cash through elective pay.

Three rules that decide whether the stack holds

Grant first, debt last.Grant and incentive dollars are what make the debt sizeable. Size the loan after you know the grants, never before.
Two programs, one cost, one time.Layering is legal. Paying for the same invoice twice is not. Keep a cost-allocation line for every dollar.
Tax-exempt does not mean no tax credit.Elective pay turns 48E into cash for churches, nonprofits and municipalities. This is the most missed line in the state.

Timing

Three clocks are already running

A consultant who knows these three dates is worth more than one who knows every program.

Dec 31, 2027

Federal solar credit

The July 4, 2026 begin-construction deadline has passed. Any solar project starting now must be placed in service by this date to claim Section 48E at all. Projects that safe-harbored have until Dec 31, 2030.

Dec 31, 2026

Illinois EV rebate cycle

Open now. $2,000 per eligible new or used EV, plus $2,000 more for low-income applicants. Vehicle price under $80,000, apply within 180 days of purchase.

2027 – 2030

Storage is the long runway

Standalone storage keeps its federal credit past the solar cliff, and CRGA commits Illinois to 3,000 MW by 2030 under 20-year contracts. This is where the next five years of work is.

How to use this. Filter, then click the program name. Every link goes to the agency's own page, not to a summary. If a program says it is not yet accepting applications, do not put it in a client's budget.

Layering rules are program-specific. Two programs may fund one project, but never the same invoice twice. Confirm cost allocation in writing with each program officer before a client signs anything.
